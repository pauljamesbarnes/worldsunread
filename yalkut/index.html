Not merely translated—made legible

Follow the Yalkut’s mind at work

Yalkut Shimoni moves rapidly among law, parable, wordplay, theology, and verses from distant Biblical books. The proposed edition preserves that movement while supplying the context a modern reader cannot reasonably be expected to reconstruct alone.
