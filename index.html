A WORLDS UNREAD EDITION

The False Baron

Marriage, Murder and the Con Man Who Hunted Wealthy Women

Translated and introduced by Paul Barnes

Coming first

A fake aristocrat. A trail of women. A murder case that refused to stay simple.

First published in Germany in 1861 as part of Der neue Pitaval, this extraordinary true-crime narrative follows a confidence trickster who built new lives through false names, false estates, false marriages — and finally found himself accused of murder.

This Worlds Unread edition brings the complete case into English in a source-verified translation, with an introduction, chronology and explanatory notes.
