Privacy

Reader-list privacy

Worlds Unread uses your email address only to send the updates you requested.

What we collect

When you join the reader list, we collect your email address and the subscription information needed to manage your consent. We do not ask for your name or postal address.

How it is used

We use it to announce Worlds Unread editions and occasionally send extracts or news about rediscovered books. We do not sell the reader list or use it for unrelated marketing.

Managing your subscription

Every reader-list email includes an unsubscribe link. You can use it at any time to stop future messages. The mailing-list service processes subscription data on behalf of Worlds Unread so subscriptions and withdrawals can be managed securely.

Questions

Privacy enquiries can be sent to hello@worldsunread.com once that address is active.
